Public Holidays Policy

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains which public holidays Nile Byte Technologies observes, how holidays interact with leave and remote work, and how employees are compensated when they work on a holiday. It applies to all employees at the Cairo (Smart Village) and Alexandria offices and to employees working remotely in Egypt. Employees permanently based outside Egypt follow the holiday schedule in their employment contract.

Public holidays are paid days off and are not deducted from annual leave. Annual leave, sick leave and other leave types are covered in the Paid Time Off and Leave Policy (POL-HR-003).

2. Official Public Holidays

Nile Byte observes the official public holidays of the Arab Republic of Egypt as announced by the Prime Minister's decrees each year. The list below reflects the standard calendar. Islamic holidays follow the lunar Hijri calendar, so their Gregorian dates change every year and are confirmed by government announcement, usually a few days in advance.

The government sometimes extends Eid holidays or moves a holiday to a Sunday or Thursday to create a long weekend. When this happens, Nile Byte follows the official decree. People Operations announces the confirmed dates by email and on the PeopleHub calendar.

3. Company Holidays

3.1 Company Day

Nile Byte was founded on 15 September 2014. Every year, 15 September is Company Day, a paid day off for all employees. If 15 September falls on a Friday or Saturday, Company Day is observed on the following Sunday. Teams on client support rotations who must work on Company Day receive a substitute day off under Section 6.

3.2 Floating Holiday

Each employee receives one floating holiday per calendar year that can be used for a religious or cultural celebration that is not an official public holiday, such as Coptic Easter Sunday, Epiphany, Christmas on 25 December, or a personal day of significance. The floating holiday must be requested in PeopleHub at least 5 working days in advance. It cannot be carried over to the next year and is not paid out if unused.

4. Weekends and Holidays

The Company's working week is Sunday to Thursday, and Friday and Saturday are the weekend. When an official holiday falls on a Friday or Saturday, employees do not receive an additional day off unless the government officially moves the holiday to a working day. Holidays that fall during a period of annual leave are not counted as leave days, and the annual leave days are restored automatically in PeopleHub.

5. Ramadan Working Hours

During the month of Ramadan, the working day for all employees is reduced to 6 hours, normally from 10:00 to 16:00, regardless of whether the employee is fasting. Core collaboration hours during Ramadan are 11:00 to 15:00. Meetings should not be scheduled after 15:30. Client-facing teams agree coverage plans with their clients before Ramadan begins. Hybrid employees may choose to work their office days remotely during the last 10 days of Ramadan, subject to team needs.

6. Working on a Public Holiday

Employees should not normally be asked to work on public holidays. Where work is essential, for example a production incident, an agreed client go-live or the on-call support rotation, the manager must obtain the employee's agreement in advance where possible and record the holiday work in PeopleHub.

For each public holiday worked, the employee chooses one of the following:

  • Double pay for the hours worked on the holiday, paid with the next month's payroll; or
  • A substitute day off, which must be taken within 30 days of the holiday, with manager approval.

Employees on the formal on-call rotation also receive the on-call allowance described in the Engineering on-call guidelines. Holiday work of less than 2 hours counts as half a day for compensation purposes.

7. Holidays and Remote Work Abroad

Employees temporarily working from another country under the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (POL-HR-005) continue to follow the Egyptian public holiday calendar. Local holidays in the other country are not additional days off.

8. Announcements and Questions

People Operations publishes the holiday calendar for the coming year in December, and updates it when official dates are confirmed. Questions should be sent to people@nilebyte.example.
