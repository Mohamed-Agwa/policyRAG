# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Nile Byte Technologies reimburses, the limits that apply, and how to submit claims. Its aim is to make sure employees are not out of pocket for legitimate business costs, while spending Company money responsibly and transparently.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees and to contractors whose contracts allow expense reimbursement. Expenses billable to clients follow this policy unless the client contract sets stricter limits, in which case the client's limits apply.

## 3. General Principles

- Spend Company money as carefully as if it were your own.
- Expenses must be necessary for Company business, reasonable in amount and supported by receipts.
- Employees may not approve their own expenses or expenses that benefit them personally.
- Expenses must comply with the gifts and hospitality rules in the Code of Conduct (POL-HR-001).

## 4. Submitting Expense Claims

### 4.1 SpendWise

All expense claims are submitted in **SpendWise**, the Company's expense management system, either in the web app or the mobile app. Each claim must include:

- the date, amount, currency and category of the expense;
- a clear business purpose, including the client or project code where applicable; and
- a photo or scan of the itemised receipt.

### 4.2 Deadlines

Expenses must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the date the expense was incurred, or within 30 days of returning from a business trip. Claims submitted after 30 days require approval from the department director. Claims submitted more than **90 days** after the expense was incurred will not be reimbursed.

### 4.3 Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every expense of **EGP 200 or more**. For expenses below EGP 200, such as small taxi fares or parking, a receipt is recommended but not required; the employee must still record the expense in SpendWise. If a receipt is lost, the employee must complete the Missing Receipt Declaration in SpendWise. Missing receipt declarations are limited to three per year.

### 4.4 Approvals

| Claim amount | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to EGP 25,000 | Line manager |
| EGP 25,001 to EGP 100,000 | Department director |
| Above EGP 100,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

Managers should review claims within **5 working days**.

### 4.5 Reimbursement Timing

Approved claims are reimbursed by bank transfer with the monthly payroll. Claims approved by the **20th of the month** are paid with that month's salary on the 25th; claims approved after the 20th are paid the following month.

## 5. Business Travel

### 5.1 Booking

All flights and hotels for business travel must be booked through the Company's travel desk at travel@nilebyte.example, at least **14 days** before departure for international trips and 7 days for domestic trips, wherever possible. Trips must be approved in advance by the line manager in SpendWise. International travel also requires director approval.

### 5.2 Air Travel

- Economy class is standard for all flights.
- **Business class** is allowed for flights with a scheduled flight time of **more than 6 hours**, with prior Vice President approval.
- Employees should book the lowest reasonable fare and avoid non-refundable tickets when plans are uncertain.
- Loyalty points earned on business travel may be kept by the employee, but must not influence the choice of airline or hotel if a cheaper option is available.

### 5.3 Hotels

| Location | Maximum rate per night |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic (Egypt) | EGP 2,500 |
| International, standard cities | USD 180 |
| International, high-cost cities (London, New York, Dubai, Zurich, Paris, Singapore) | USD 250 |

Rates exclude taxes. Employees staying with friends or family instead of a hotel may claim a flat **EGP 500 per night** (domestic) or **USD 40 per night** (international) as an accommodation allowance.

### 5.4 Per Diem for Meals

Employees on overnight business travel receive a daily meal allowance (per diem) instead of claiming individual meal receipts:

| Travel type | Per diem |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic overnight travel | EGP 900 per day |
| International travel, standard cities | USD 60 per day |
| International travel, high-cost cities | USD 85 per day |

On the day of departure and the day of return, 50% of the per diem applies. If a meal is provided, for example by the client or at a conference, the per diem is reduced by 30% for each provided lunch or dinner.

### 5.5 Ground Transportation

- Ride-hailing services, taxis, metro and airport transfers for business purposes are reimbursable.
- Car rental requires manager approval and must be an economy or compact model.
- Employees using their own car for business travel are reimbursed at **EGP 5 per kilometre**, plus tolls and parking. Commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable.

### 5.6 Travel Advances

Employees travelling internationally may request a travel advance of up to **70% of the estimated trip cost** in SpendWise at least 7 working days before departure. Advances must be settled with a full expense report within 15 days of returning. Unspent amounts are returned to Finance or deducted from the next payroll.

### 5.7 Visas, Vaccinations and Travel Insurance

Visa fees, required vaccinations and travel insurance for business trips are reimbursable. The Company's travel insurance covers medical emergencies abroad; employees should carry the insurance certificate provided by the travel desk.

## 6. Client Entertainment and Team Meals

- Client meals and entertainment are reimbursable up to **EGP 1,500 per person**, including the employee, when there is a clear business purpose.
- The claim must list the names and companies of all attendees.
- The most senior Nile Byte employee present must pay and submit the claim.
- Team celebrations require prior approval of the budget by the department director.

## 7. Corporate Cards

Employees who travel on business more than **4 times per year**, or who regularly entertain clients, may request a corporate credit card. Corporate cards must be used only for business expenses; personal use is prohibited, even if repaid. Card transactions must be reconciled in SpendWise with receipts by the 5th of the following month.

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursed:

- alcoholic beverages;
- traffic fines, parking fines and penalties;
- personal entertainment, such as films, minibar items and in-room entertainment;
- upgrades to flights, hotel rooms or rental cars that are not covered by this policy;
- personal grooming, clothing and toiletries;
- costs of family members or friends accompanying the employee;
- lost or stolen personal items; and
- expenses for which another party, such as a client or conference organiser, has already paid.

## 9. Other Reimbursable Expenses

Other reimbursable business expenses include:

- learning and certification costs approved under the Benefits and Wellbeing Policy (POL-HR-008);
- the home office allowance under the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (POL-HR-005);
- software or online services needed for work, if approved by IT in advance under the Acceptable Use of IT Resources Policy (POL-IT-002); and
- shipping and courier costs for business documents or equipment.

## 10. Foreign Currency

Expenses in foreign currency are converted to Egyptian pounds at the exchange rate on the employee's card statement, or at the Central Bank of Egypt rate on the date of the expense if paid in cash.

## 11. Misuse

Submitting false, inflated or duplicate claims is fraud and will lead to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal under the Code of Conduct (POL-HR-001), and recovery of the amounts involved. Finance audits a random sample of claims every month.

## 12. Questions

Questions about this policy should be sent to Finance at finance@nilebyte.example. Questions about travel bookings should be sent to travel@nilebyte.example.
