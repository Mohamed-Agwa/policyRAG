# Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Nile Byte Technologies wants employees to rest, recover and attend to personal and family matters. This policy explains the types of leave available, how much leave employees receive, and how to request it. It applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Interns and contractors are covered by the terms of their own agreements.

The leave year runs from **1 January to 31 December**. Leave is requested, approved and tracked in **PeopleHub**, the Company's HR system. Public holidays are covered separately in the Public Holidays Policy (POL-HR-004) and are not deducted from annual leave.

## 2. Annual Leave

### 2.1 Entitlement

| Employee group | Annual leave per year |
| --- | --- |
| Less than 10 years of total service, and under age 50 | 21 working days |
| 10 or more years of total service, or aged 50 or over | 30 working days |
| Employees in their first year of service | 15 working days, rising to 21 after 6 months of service |

Total service includes previous employment that the employee can document through social insurance records. The higher entitlement starts from the beginning of the leave year in which the employee reaches 10 years of service or turns 50.

### 2.2 Accrual for New Joiners

Annual leave accrues monthly at 1.75 days per month of service for employees on the 21-day entitlement. New employees may not take annual leave during their **first 3 months** (the probation period), except for casual leave described in Section 4 or with special approval from People Operations for exceptional circumstances.

### 2.3 Requesting Annual Leave

- Leave of **3 or more consecutive working days** must be requested in PeopleHub at least **10 working days** in advance.
- Shorter leave must be requested at least **2 working days** in advance.
- Managers should respond to leave requests within 3 working days. A request that receives no response within 3 working days should be escalated to the next-level manager.
- Managers may decline or ask employees to reschedule leave because of critical business needs, such as a client release, but must offer alternative dates.
- During the December holiday period and the two weeks before major client releases, teams should coordinate so that at least half of each team remains available.

### 2.4 Mandatory Rest

Each employee must take at least **one block of 6 consecutive working days** of annual leave every year, as required by Egyptian labour law and to support wellbeing. People Operations sends a reminder to employees who have not booked such a block by 30 September.

### 2.5 Carryover and Forfeiture

- Employees may carry over up to **7 unused annual leave days** into the next leave year.
- Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of the following year. Any carried-over days not used by that date are forfeited.
- Unused days beyond the 7-day carryover limit are forfeited on 31 December, unless the employee's leave was cancelled by the Company for business reasons, in which case the cancelled days are carried over in full and must be used within 6 months.
- Unused annual leave is not paid out in cash, except on termination of employment, when accrued and unused leave for the current year plus any valid carryover is paid at the employee's basic salary rate.

### 2.6 Sickness During Annual Leave

If an employee becomes sick during annual leave and provides a medical certificate covering the sick days, those days are converted to sick leave and the annual leave days are restored.

## 3. Sick Leave

### 3.1 Reporting Sickness

An employee who is unable to work because of illness must inform their manager **before 10:00** on the first day of absence, by phone or chat, and record the absence in PeopleHub on the day they return.

### 3.2 Medical Certificates

- Employees may self-certify sick leave of up to **3 consecutive working days** without a medical certificate.
- Absences of **4 or more consecutive working days** require a medical certificate, uploaded to PeopleHub within 2 working days of returning to work.
- Self-certified sick leave is limited to **8 days per calendar year**; after that, a medical certificate is required for every sick day.

### 3.3 Sick Pay

| Period of sick leave in a calendar year | Pay |
| --- | --- |
| First 30 working days | 100% of salary |
| Next 60 working days | 75% of salary |
| Beyond 90 working days | Handled under social insurance rules and reviewed case by case |

Employees with long-term illness should contact People Operations to discuss phased returns, adjusted duties or other accommodations under the Anti-Harassment and Equal Opportunity Policy (POL-HR-002).

## 4. Casual (Emergency) Leave

Employees may take up to **6 days of casual leave per year** for unexpected urgent matters, such as a sudden family emergency, without advance approval. Casual leave:

- is counted as part of the annual leave entitlement, not in addition to it;
- may be taken for a maximum of **2 consecutive days** at a time; and
- must be reported to the manager as soon as possible on the same day and recorded in PeopleHub within 2 working days.

Casual leave may be used during the probation period.

## 5. Family Leave

### 5.1 Maternity Leave

Female employees are entitled to **4 months of fully paid maternity leave**, which can start up to 1 month before the expected date of delivery. Maternity leave may be taken up to three times during employment with the Company. After returning, a nursing employee is entitled to a **one-hour reduction** of her daily working hours, which can be taken at the start or end of the day, for 24 months after the birth. Employees should notify People Operations of their intended start date at least 8 weeks before the leave begins.

### 5.2 Paternity Leave

Male employees are entitled to **5 working days of paid paternity leave**, to be taken within 30 days of the birth or adoption of a child.

### 5.3 Childcare Leave (Unpaid)

After maternity leave, an employee may request unpaid childcare leave of up to **2 years** at a time, up to twice during employment. The employee's role, or an equivalent role at the same level, is guaranteed on return.

## 6. Other Paid Leave

| Leave type | Entitlement | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bereavement leave | 3 working days | Death of a spouse, parent, child, sibling or grandparent |
| Bereavement leave (extended family) | 1 working day | Death of an aunt, uncle, cousin or parent-in-law |
| Marriage leave | 5 working days | Once during employment, within 3 months of the marriage date |
| Hajj or pilgrimage leave | Up to 30 calendar days | Once during employment, after 5 years of continuous service |
| Exam leave | Up to 5 working days per year | For exams in a degree or certification approved by the manager |
| Jury, court or official summons | As required | A copy of the summons must be provided |
| Blood donation | Half a working day | Proof of donation required |

Requests for these types of leave should be made in PeopleHub with the appropriate supporting document, such as a death certificate, marriage contract or exam schedule. Bereavement leave can be recorded after the employee returns.

## 7. Unpaid Leave

Employees who have completed at least 1 year of service may request unpaid leave of up to **3 months** for personal reasons such as further study, family care or travel. Unpaid leave requires approval from the employee's manager and People Operations, and requests should be made at least 1 month in advance. During unpaid leave, salary and leave accrual stop, and the employee's medical insurance continues only if the employee pays the full premium.

## 8. Leave and Remote Work

Working from another location is not leave. Rules for working from home or temporarily from abroad are in the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (POL-HR-005). Employees must not work while on any type of leave, and managers must not contact employees on leave except in a genuine emergency.

## 9. Recording and Abuse

All leave must be recorded accurately in PeopleHub. Taking leave without approval, falsifying medical certificates or misusing sick leave is a disciplinary matter under the Code of Conduct (POL-HR-001). Unauthorised absence of more than 10 consecutive days, or 20 days in a year, may lead to termination in line with Egyptian labour law.

## 10. Questions

Questions about leave balances and entitlements should be sent to People Operations at people@nilebyte.example. Employees can see their current balances at any time on the PeopleHub dashboard.
