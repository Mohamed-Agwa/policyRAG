# Performance Review Policy

## 1. Purpose

Nile Byte Technologies believes that clear goals, regular feedback and fair recognition help people grow and help the Company deliver excellent work for clients. This policy describes how goals are set, how performance is reviewed and rated, how reviews connect to pay and promotion, and how performance concerns are handled.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probation period. Employees on probation are reviewed under the Onboarding and Probation Policy (POL-HR-006). Employees who joined after 1 October are included in the following year's annual review cycle, but receive a mid-cycle check-in.

## 3. The Performance Year

The performance year runs from **1 January to 31 December** and includes the following milestones:

| Period | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| January | Annual goals set for the new year; previous year's reviews finalised |
| End of each quarter | Quarterly goal (OKR) review and update |
| June | Mid-year check-in |
| December | Self-assessment and peer feedback |
| Early January | Manager review, calibration and final ratings |
| April | Salary increases and annual bonuses take effect |

## 4. Goals

Each employee agrees **3 to 5 annual goals** with their manager in January, recorded in PeopleHub. Goals should be specific, measurable and connected to team and Company objectives. Engineering teams also use quarterly objectives and key results (OKRs), which are reviewed at the end of each quarter and can be adjusted as priorities change.

At least one goal should relate to personal development, such as learning a new technology, earning a certification or mentoring a colleague. Learning activities may be funded through the learning budget described in the Benefits and Wellbeing Policy (POL-HR-008).

## 5. Continuous Feedback

### 5.1 One-to-One Meetings

Managers hold a one-to-one meeting with each team member **at least every two weeks**. One-to-ones focus on progress, obstacles, wellbeing and development, and should not be cancelled more than once in a row.

### 5.2 Feedback Between Colleagues

Employees can give and request feedback at any time using the feedback feature in PeopleHub. Feedback should be specific, respectful and focused on behaviour and results, not personality.

## 6. Mid-Year Check-in

In June, each employee and manager hold a structured mid-year conversation to review progress against goals, discuss development and agree any changes for the second half of the year. The mid-year check-in does not produce a rating, but managers must raise any significant performance concerns in writing at this point.

## 7. Annual Review

### 7.1 Inputs

The annual review uses:

- the employee's **self-assessment**, submitted in PeopleHub by **15 December**;
- feedback from **2 to 4 peers** chosen by the employee and approved by the manager;
- for people managers, an anonymous upward feedback survey from their team members; and
- the manager's own assessment of goal results and behaviours aligned with the Company values described in the Code of Conduct (POL-HR-001).

### 7.2 Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Exceptional | Consistently far exceeds expectations and has impact well beyond the role |
| 4 | Exceeds | Regularly exceeds expectations for the role and level |
| 3 | Solid | Fully meets expectations; the expected rating for most employees |
| 2 | Developing | Meets some expectations; improvement is needed in specific areas |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | Does not meet expectations; a performance improvement plan is required |

The Company does not use forced distribution. However, ratings of 5 are expected to be rare.

### 7.3 Calibration

Before ratings are shared with employees, managers in each department meet in **calibration sessions** facilitated by People Operations to compare proposed ratings across teams, reduce bias and ensure consistent standards. Managers may not communicate final ratings until calibration is complete.

### 7.4 Review Conversation

Each employee receives a written review and a review meeting with their manager by **31 January**. The employee can add comments to the review in PeopleHub within 10 working days of the meeting.

## 8. Pay and Bonuses

### 8.1 Salary Review

Salary increases are based on the annual rating, the employee's position in the salary band for their level, and the Company's overall budget. Increases take effect on **1 April** each year and are communicated in writing in March. Employees rated 1 are not eligible for a merit increase.

### 8.2 Annual Bonus

Employees on the bonus plan receive an annual bonus that depends on Company results and individual rating. The bonus is paid with the **April** payroll. To receive the bonus, employees must be employed on the payment date and must not be serving notice of resignation, unless the employee is retiring.

## 9. Promotions

Promotions are considered **twice a year, in April and October**. A promotion requires that the employee consistently performs at the next level, as described in the career framework for their job family, and that there is a business need for the role. Managers submit promotion cases with supporting evidence, which are reviewed by a promotion committee made up of directors and People Operations. Promotions normally come with a salary increase of at least 10%.

## 10. Managing Underperformance

### 10.1 Performance Improvement Plan

When an employee's performance does not meet expectations, the manager first discusses the concerns informally and offers support. If performance does not improve, or after a rating of 1, the manager, with People Operations, puts in place a **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)**. A PIP:

- lasts **60 calendar days**;
- sets out specific, measurable objectives and the support the Company will provide;
- includes check-in meetings at least every two weeks; and
- ends with a written outcome: successful completion, an extension of up to 30 days, or further action.

### 10.2 Outcome

If the objectives of the PIP are not met, the Company may move the employee to a more suitable role where one exists or begin the formal disciplinary or termination procedure in line with Egyptian labour law. Employees on a PIP may bring a colleague to formal meetings.

## 11. Appeals

An employee who believes their annual rating is unfair may appeal in writing to People Operations within **15 working days** of the review meeting. The appeal is reviewed by the manager's manager together with a People Operations partner, who may change the rating. The appeal decision is communicated within 20 working days.

## 12. Records

Reviews, ratings and PIP documents are stored in PeopleHub and are visible only to the employee, their current manager and People Operations. Records are retained for the duration of employment and for 5 years after employment ends, in line with the Data Privacy and Protection Policy (POL-IT-003).
