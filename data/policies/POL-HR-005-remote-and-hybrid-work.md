# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

Nile Byte Technologies uses a hybrid model that combines the focus and flexibility of remote work with the collaboration of time together in the office. This policy explains who can work remotely, how often, which expectations apply, what equipment and allowances the Company provides, and the rules for working temporarily from abroad.

## 2. Scope and Eligibility

This policy applies to all employees whose role can be performed remotely. Roles that require physical presence, such as office operations, reception and hardware lab work, are excluded unless otherwise agreed. Employees on probation follow the hybrid schedule from their first week, but may be asked by their manager to spend additional days in the office during their first month to support onboarding (see the Onboarding and Probation Policy, POL-HR-006).

Remote work is a working arrangement, not a contractual right. The Company may change an individual's arrangement where business needs or performance require it, giving at least 2 weeks' notice except in urgent cases.

## 3. Work Models

| Model | Description | Approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hybrid (default) | 3 days per week in the office and 2 days remote | Standard for all eligible employees |
| Office-based | 5 days per week in the office | Employee's choice |
| Fully remote | Works remotely every day, visiting the office for planned events | Exception approved by the department Vice President and People Operations |

### 3.1 Hybrid Schedule

Hybrid employees work in the office **3 days per week**. **Monday and Wednesday are anchor days**, when everyone in the hybrid model is expected in the office so that teams can collaborate in person. The third office day is chosen by each team and agreed with the team lead. Employees record their planned office days in the PeopleHub desk-booking tool by Thursday of the previous week.

If a public holiday falls on an anchor day, employees do not need to make up the office day that week.

### 3.2 Fully Remote Exceptions

Fully remote arrangements are approved in exceptional circumstances, such as a health condition, caring responsibilities or relocation to a city without a Nile Byte office. Fully remote employees must live in Egypt, unless their contract states otherwise, and must attend the office for the quarterly planning days and the annual Company Day. The Company pays reasonable travel costs for these visits under the Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-FIN-001). Fully remote arrangements are reviewed every 12 months.

## 4. Working Hours and Availability

- The standard working week is Sunday to Thursday, 40 hours per week.
- **Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Cairo time.** Employees must be available for meetings and collaboration during core hours, whether in the office or remote. Outside core hours, employees can arrange their start and finish times with their team.
- During core hours, remote employees should respond to messages from colleagues within **1 business hour** and keep their chat status up to date.
- Cameras should be on during team meetings and client calls unless bandwidth or a personal reason makes this impractical.
- Ramadan working hours are described in the Public Holidays Policy (POL-HR-004).

Overtime must be approved in advance by the manager. Employees should not routinely work in the evenings or at weekends, and managers should avoid sending non-urgent messages outside working hours.

## 5. Home Workspace

Employees working remotely must have a safe, quiet and private workspace with a reliable internet connection of at least **20 Mbps download**. The workspace should allow confidential conversations to take place without being overheard. Employees are responsible for keeping their home workspace safe, and should report any work-related injury at home to People Operations within 24 hours.

Ergonomic guidance, including desk height, chair position and screen distance, is available on the intranet. Employees can request a free ergonomic video consultation through People Operations.

## 6. Equipment and Allowances

### 6.1 Equipment

The Company provides each employee with a laptop, a headset and, for hybrid and fully remote employees, **one external monitor** for home use. Company equipment remains Company property, must be returned when employment ends and must be used in line with the Acceptable Use of IT Resources Policy (POL-IT-002). Faults must be reported to the IT Service Desk.

### 6.2 Internet Stipend

Hybrid and fully remote employees receive a monthly internet stipend of **EGP 600**, paid with their salary. Office-based employees do not receive the stipend.

### 6.3 Home Office Allowance

After completing probation, hybrid and fully remote employees can claim a **one-time home office allowance of up to EGP 4,000** for items such as a desk, an office chair or a desk lamp. The allowance is claimed through SpendWise with receipts within 6 months of becoming eligible. Items purchased with the allowance belong to the employee. The allowance can be claimed again after 3 years.

## 7. Security When Working Remotely

Remote work must meet the same security standards as office work. In particular:

- Employees must connect through the Company **VPN** when accessing internal systems, and must always use the VPN on public or shared Wi-Fi networks such as cafés, hotels and co-working spaces.
- Laptops must be locked when unattended, even at home. The screen locks automatically after 5 minutes.
- Confidential and Restricted information must not be printed at home. Paper documents brought home must be stored securely and shredded or returned to the office for disposal.
- Video calls involving Confidential information must not be taken in public places.
- Family members and friends must not use Company devices.

Full security rules are in the Information Security Policy (POL-IT-001).

## 8. Working Temporarily from Abroad

Employees may work from outside Egypt for up to **20 working days per calendar year**, for example to extend a holiday or visit family. Working from abroad:

- must be requested in PeopleHub at least **15 working days** in advance and approved by the manager and People Operations;
- requires confirmation from the IT Service Desk that the destination is permitted for VPN access; working from countries subject to international sanctions is not permitted;
- may be taken in one block or several shorter periods, but not more than 10 consecutive working days without additional Vice President approval;
- does not change the employee's working hours, which remain aligned to Cairo core hours; and
- does not include any travel, accommodation or living costs, which are the employee's responsibility.

Days spent working abroad on Company business trips do not count toward the 20-day limit. Employees must not work from abroad while on any type of leave.

## 9. Performance and Conduct

Remote and hybrid employees are evaluated on the same goals and standards as office-based employees, as described in the Performance Review Policy (POL-HR-007). Managers hold at least one one-to-one meeting every two weeks with each team member, whether in person or by video. Persistent unavailability during core hours, failure to follow the anchor-day schedule or misuse of remote work may lead to the remote arrangement being withdrawn, in addition to any disciplinary measures under the Code of Conduct (POL-HR-001).

## 10. Questions

Questions about remote work arrangements should be directed to the employee's manager or to People Operations at people@nilebyte.example. Questions about VPN access and equipment should be directed to the IT Service Desk at helpdesk@nilebyte.example.
