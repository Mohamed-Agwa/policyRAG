# Onboarding and Probation Policy

## 1. Purpose

A good start helps new employees become productive, confident and connected to their team. This policy describes what happens before a new employee joins, during the first weeks, and through the probation period, and explains how probation is reviewed and confirmed.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all new permanent and fixed-term employees, and to existing employees who move to a new role in a different department, who complete the role-specific parts of onboarding. Interns follow a shorter onboarding programme managed by the Talent team.

## 3. Before the First Day

### 3.1 Employment Documents

New employees must submit the following documents to People Operations through the PeopleHub onboarding portal **at least 5 working days before their start date**:

- a copy of the national ID card (or passport and valid work permit for non-Egyptian employees);
- the social insurance number and, where applicable, the social insurance printout from the previous employer;
- a recent criminal record certificate (issued within the last 3 months);
- military service status certificate, for male Egyptian employees;
- graduation certificate and any professional certifications required for the role;
- two personal photographs; and
- bank account details for salary payment.

Employment cannot start until the signed employment contract and these documents are received. People Operations registers new employees with the National Organization for Social Insurance within the legal deadline.

### 3.2 Preparation by the Company

Before the first day:

- the hiring manager assigns an **onboarding buddy**, an experienced colleague from the same or a nearby team who is not the employee's manager;
- the IT Service Desk prepares the laptop, accounts and access, so that the employee has a working laptop on day one;
- the hiring manager prepares a 30-60-90 day plan with clear first goals; and
- People Operations sends a welcome email with the start time, office location, parking information and the first-day schedule.

## 4. The First Week

The first day starts at **09:30** at the Smart Village or Alexandria office. Everyone attends the first week in the office, regardless of their eventual work model, so they can meet their team in person.

| Day | Activities |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Welcome session with People Operations, laptop handover and setup with IT, office tour, lunch with the team and buddy |
| Day 2 | Company overview, values and Code of Conduct session, introduction to PeopleHub, SpendWise and other internal tools |
| Day 3 | Security and privacy induction, development environment setup (engineering roles), meeting key stakeholders |
| Day 4 | Shadowing team rituals such as stand-ups and planning, first small task |
| Day 5 | First one-to-one with the manager to review the 30-60-90 day plan, end-of-week check-in with the buddy |

### 4.1 Pay Day

Salaries are paid monthly on the **25th of each month**, or on the last working day before the 25th if it falls on a weekend or public holiday. Employees who join after the 15th of the month receive their first month's pro-rated salary with the following month's payroll.

## 5. Mandatory Training

New employees must complete the following within their **first 30 days**:

- Information security awareness training (see the Information Security Policy, POL-IT-001);
- Data privacy training (see the Data Privacy and Protection Policy, POL-IT-003);
- Anti-harassment training (see POL-HR-002);
- Code of Conduct acknowledgement in PeopleHub (see POL-HR-001); and
- Health and safety induction, including the office evacuation procedure.

Access to client production systems is granted only after security and privacy training are complete.

## 6. The Onboarding Buddy

The buddy helps the new employee find their way around, explains how the team works and answers everyday questions. Buddies meet the new employee at least **twice a week during the first month** and once a week during the second and third months. Buddies receive recognition in their own performance reviews for this contribution.

## 7. Probation Period

### 7.1 Length

All new employees complete a probation period of **3 months** from their start date. The probation period cannot be extended beyond 3 months, in line with Egyptian labour law. Employees who move internally to a new role do not serve a new probation period.

### 7.2 During Probation

During probation, employees:

- follow the hybrid schedule but may be asked to spend more office days during the first month;
- cannot take annual leave, except casual leave and leave approved by People Operations in exceptional circumstances (see the Paid Time Off and Leave Policy, POL-HR-003);
- are not yet eligible for the home office allowance (see the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy, POL-HR-005); and
- are covered by medical insurance from their first day (see the Benefits and Wellbeing Policy, POL-HR-008).

### 7.3 Check-ins and Reviews

| Milestone | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| End of week 1 | First one-to-one with the manager to agree the 30-60-90 day plan |
| End of month 1 | Informal check-in with the manager and buddy on progress and support needs |
| End of month 2 | **Formal probation review**: the manager records progress against the plan in PeopleHub and gives written feedback, including any areas that must improve |
| Before the end of month 3 | Final probation decision recorded in PeopleHub by the manager and confirmed by People Operations |

If performance or conduct concerns arise, the manager must raise them clearly and in writing at the latest at the month-2 review, so that the employee has a fair opportunity to improve.

### 7.4 Confirmation

When probation is passed successfully, People Operations sends a confirmation letter, and the employee becomes eligible for all benefits that depend on completing probation, including annual leave and the home office allowance. If the manager does not record a decision by the end of the probation period, the employee is considered confirmed.

### 7.5 Ending Employment During Probation

If the Company decides that the employee is not suitable for the role, employment may be ended before the end of the probation period. Either the employee or the Company may end employment during probation by giving **7 calendar days' written notice**. The decision must be approved by People Operations, and the employee receives written reasons.

## 8. Feedback on Onboarding

At the end of month 1 and month 3, new employees receive a short anonymous survey about their onboarding experience. People Operations reviews the results every quarter to improve the programme.

## 9. Questions

Questions about onboarding should be sent to people@nilebyte.example. Laptop and access problems during the first week should be reported to the IT Service Desk at helpdesk@nilebyte.example.
