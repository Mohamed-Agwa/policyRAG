# Acceptable Use of IT Resources Policy

## 1. Purpose

Nile Byte Technologies provides laptops, software, accounts, networks and online services so that employees can do their work effectively. This policy explains how these resources may and may not be used. It complements the Information Security Policy (POL-IT-001), which sets the technical security requirements.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors and interns, and to all Company IT resources, including laptops, monitors, phones, email, chat, source code repositories, cloud accounts, internal applications, office networks and Company accounts on third-party services. It also applies to personal devices when they are used to access Company systems.

## 3. General Principles

- Company IT resources are provided primarily for business use.
- Users are responsible for all activity performed with their accounts.
- Use must be lawful, respectful and consistent with the Code of Conduct (POL-HR-001).
- Users must not attempt to bypass security controls, even to get work done faster.

## 4. Personal Use

Limited personal use of Company IT resources is permitted, provided that it:

- is occasional and brief, such as checking personal email or online banking during a break;
- does not interfere with work or with other users;
- does not consume significant bandwidth or storage, for example streaming video for long periods or storing personal photo libraries; and
- does not break any rule in this policy.

Personal files stored on Company devices are not backed up and may be deleted when the device is returned or reimaged.

## 5. Prohibited Use

The following are prohibited on Company IT resources and when using Company accounts:

- accessing, storing or sharing illegal, pornographic, violent, hateful or discriminatory material;
- harassing, threatening or bullying others (see POL-HR-002);
- downloading, sharing or using pirated software, films, music or other content that infringes copyright;
- cryptocurrency mining or running personal commercial activities;
- installing or using hacking tools, network scanners or password crackers, except by authorised security staff for approved testing;
- disabling or tampering with endpoint protection, encryption, the management agent or monitoring tools;
- connecting unauthorised devices, such as personal routers or USB storage, to Company equipment or networks;
- sending spam, chain letters or unsolicited bulk messages; and
- using Company resources for political campaigning or fundraising unrelated to Company-approved charitable activities.

## 6. Software and Services

### 6.1 Approved Software

Only software from the **Company software catalogue** may be installed on Company laptops. The catalogue is available in the self-service portal and includes development tools, browsers, productivity applications and approved browser extensions. Software not in the catalogue must be requested through the IT Service Desk; requests are reviewed by IT and Information Security for licensing and security, normally within **5 working days**.

### 6.2 Open-Source Software

Open-source libraries may be used in development, subject to the Engineering open-source approval process, which checks licences and known vulnerabilities before a library is used in client deliverables.

### 6.3 Cloud Services and File Sharing

- Company information must be stored only in approved Company systems, such as the Company document workspace, approved source code repositories and approved cloud accounts.
- **Personal cloud storage accounts** (for example, personal Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud), personal email and personal messaging apps must **not** be used to store or send Company or client information.
- Files containing Confidential information shared with external parties must use the Company's secure file-sharing tool with expiring links.

## 7. Generative AI Tools

Generative AI tools can boost productivity, but they can also leak confidential information and produce inaccurate output.

### 7.1 Approved Tools

Only AI tools listed as approved in the software catalogue may be used for Company work. The approved list includes the Company's enterprise AI assistant and approved AI coding assistants configured so that Company data is not used to train the vendor's models. Employees may request new AI tools through the IT Service Desk.

### 7.2 Rules for Using AI Tools

- **Confidential and Restricted information**, including client source code, client data, personal data and credentials, must **never** be entered into AI tools that are not on the approved list.
- Even with approved tools, client data may be used only where the client contract allows it.
- Employees are responsible for reviewing and testing all AI-generated content and code before using it. AI output must be checked for accuracy, security vulnerabilities and licensing issues.
- AI-generated content shared with clients must be reviewed by a person and, where the client contract requires it, disclosed as AI-assisted.

## 8. Email and Chat

- Company email and chat must be used professionally. Messages may be retained and reviewed in line with Section 10.
- Do not use email or chat to send passwords or Restricted information; use the password manager's secure sharing feature or the approved secure file-sharing tool instead.
- Auto-forwarding Company email to personal email accounts is prohibited.
- Group chat channels should be used for work topics; social channels are available for informal conversation and must still follow the Code of Conduct.

## 9. Personal Devices (BYOD)

Employees may use personal phones and tablets to access Company email, chat and calendar only after enrolling them in the Company **mobile device management (MDM)** application. The MDM application creates a separate work profile; the Company can see and wipe only the work profile, not personal photos, messages or apps. Personal laptops must not be used to access Company systems except the self-service portal and PeopleHub through a browser.

Employees must remove the work profile, or allow it to be removed, when they leave the Company or replace the device.

## 10. Monitoring and Privacy

To protect its systems and comply with the law, Nile Byte monitors the use of Company IT resources. Monitoring includes security logs, network traffic for threats, email filtering for malware and phishing, and device compliance status. Monitoring is carried out by authorised IT and Information Security staff only, and is proportionate to its security purpose. The Company does not monitor the content of personal work profiles on personal devices.

Access to an individual employee's email or files without their knowledge requires written approval from the CISO and the Head of People Operations, and is permitted only for investigating a suspected serious policy breach, a security incident or a legal requirement. Monitoring data is processed according to the Data Privacy and Protection Policy (POL-IT-003).

## 11. Social Media

Employees may use personal social media accounts, but must not:

- post Confidential or Restricted information, including client names, project details or screenshots of internal systems, without permission;
- present personal opinions as the Company's position; or
- post content that harasses colleagues or breaches the Code of Conduct.

Employees who mention that they work at Nile Byte should add that their views are their own. Official Company accounts are managed only by the Marketing team.

## 12. Returning Equipment

When employment ends, employees must return all Company equipment, including the laptop, monitor, headset, security key and access badge, on or before their last working day. The final settlement may be withheld until all equipment is returned. Equipment issued for home use under the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (POL-HR-005) can be returned to either office or collected by courier at the Company's expense.

## 13. Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to suspension of access and disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct (POL-HR-001). Illegal activity may be reported to the authorities. Questions about this policy should be directed to the IT Service Desk at helpdesk@nilebyte.example.
