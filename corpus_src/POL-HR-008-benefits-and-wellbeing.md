# Benefits and Wellbeing Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes the benefits that Nile Byte Technologies provides to support the health, financial security, learning and wellbeing of its employees. It applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees. Part-time employees receive the same insurance benefits and pro-rated cash allowances. Interns and contractors receive the benefits set out in their agreements. Benefits start on the employee's first day of work unless this policy states otherwise.

Benefits described in this policy may be changed when insurance contracts are renewed each year on 1 July. People Operations announces any changes at least 30 days in advance.

## 2. Medical Insurance

### 2.1 Coverage

All employees are covered by the Company's private medical insurance from their **first day of employment**, including during probation. The plan covers outpatient visits, hospitalisation, surgery, maternity care, laboratory tests, scans and prescription medicines at the insurer's network of hospitals, clinics and pharmacies across Egypt.

### 2.2 Dependants

Employees may add the following dependants to the medical plan at no cost to the employee:

- a spouse; and
- children up to the age of **24**, or older if the child has a permanent disability.

Parents of the employee may be added at the employee's own cost, at the Company's group rate, through monthly payroll deduction. Dependants must be added within 30 days of joining or within 30 days of a qualifying event such as marriage or birth; otherwise they can only be added at the next annual renewal on 1 July.

### 2.3 Plan Classes

| Employee level | Plan class | Hospital room |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Levels 1 to 3 | Class B | Shared room |
| Levels 4 to 6 | Class A | Private room |
| Directors and above | Class A+ | Private suite, international emergency coverage |

### 2.4 Co-payment and Claims

Outpatient visits within the network have a co-payment of **10%**, capped at EGP 150 per visit. Hospitalisation within the network has no co-payment. Treatment outside the network is reimbursed at the network rate after the employee submits a claim with original receipts to the insurer within **60 days** of treatment.

### 2.5 Annual Health Check

Employees aged 35 and over are entitled to a free **annual health check** at a partner clinic. Employees under 35 are entitled to one every two years.

## 3. Dental and Optical Benefits

- **Dental:** Dental treatment is reimbursed up to **EGP 5,000 per person per calendar year**, for the employee and each covered dependant. Cosmetic procedures such as whitening and veneers are not covered.
- **Optical:** Eye tests are fully covered. Glasses or contact lenses are reimbursed up to **EGP 3,000 per person every two calendar years**.

Claims are submitted through the insurer's mobile app with receipts.

## 4. Life and Disability Insurance

All employees are covered by group life insurance paying **24 times the monthly basic salary** to the employee's nominated beneficiaries in the event of death from any cause. Accidental death or permanent total disability caused by an accident pays an additional 24 times the monthly basic salary. Employees should keep their beneficiary nomination up to date in PeopleHub.

## 5. Social Insurance and Retirement

### 5.1 Social Insurance

Nile Byte registers all employees with the National Organization for Social Insurance and pays the employer contributions required by law. The employee's share is deducted from salary each month.

### 5.2 Nile Byte Savings Fund

Employees who have completed probation can join the voluntary **Nile Byte Savings Fund**, a private pension and savings plan.

- Employees may contribute between 1% and 10% of their basic salary each month.
- The Company **matches 100% of employee contributions up to 5%** of basic salary.
- Employee contributions and their returns always belong to the employee.
- Company contributions vest after **3 years** of fund membership. Employees who leave before 3 years receive only their own contributions and returns.

Employees can join or change their contribution rate twice a year, in January and July.

## 6. Allowances

| Allowance | Amount | Eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meal allowance | EGP 1,200 per month | All employees, paid with salary |
| Transportation allowance | EGP 1,000 per month | Office-based and hybrid employees |
| Internet stipend | EGP 600 per month | Hybrid and fully remote employees (see POL-HR-005) |
| Mobile phone allowance | EGP 400 per month | Employees required by their role to be reachable by phone, such as on-call engineers and account managers |

Allowances are pro-rated for part-time employees and for partial months.

## 7. Learning and Development

### 7.1 Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of **EGP 15,000** for courses, books, conferences and online learning platforms related to their current or future role at Nile Byte. The budget resets on 1 January and does not carry over. Requests are approved by the manager in PeopleHub before the expense is incurred, and receipts are submitted through SpendWise under the Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-FIN-001).

### 7.2 Certifications

The cost of professional certification exams relevant to the employee's role (for example, cloud provider, security or project management certifications) is reimbursed in full **if the employee passes the exam**. Reimbursement is outside the annual learning budget. A maximum of two certification attempts per year are reimbursed.

### 7.3 Study Leave and Sponsored Degrees

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid exam leave per year (see POL-HR-003). Employees with at least 2 years of service may apply for partial sponsorship of a master's degree relevant to their role. Sponsored employees agree to remain with the Company for 2 years after completing the degree, or repay a pro-rated share of the sponsorship.

## 8. Wellbeing

### 8.1 Employee Assistance Programme

The confidential **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)** offers employees and their immediate family members up to **6 free counselling sessions per person per year** with licensed counsellors, in Arabic or English, in person or online. The EAP also provides legal and financial guidance by phone. The Company receives only anonymised usage statistics and never learns who used the service.

### 8.2 Fitness Subsidy

The Company reimburses **50% of gym, sports club or fitness class fees, up to EGP 750 per month**. Claims are submitted quarterly through SpendWise with receipts.

### 8.3 Wellness Activities

Nile Byte organises regular wellness activities, including a monthly football match, yoga sessions at the Smart Village office, and the annual family day. Participation is voluntary.

## 9. Family Support

- **New baby gift:** EGP 5,000 for the birth or adoption of a child.
- **Nursing room:** Both offices have a private nursing room.
- **Childcare:** Employees with children under 4 years old receive a childcare allowance of **EGP 1,500 per month per child**, up to two children.

Paid family leave is described in the Paid Time Off and Leave Policy (POL-HR-003).

## 10. Employee Discounts

Employees receive discounts negotiated with partners, including mobile network plans, selected restaurants near the offices, and consumer electronics stores. The current list is published on the intranet benefits page.

## 11. Leaving the Company

Medical, dental, optical and life insurance coverage ends on the last day of employment. Unused allowances are not paid out. The Savings Fund balance is paid out according to the vesting rules in Section 5.2.

## 12. Questions

Questions about benefits should be sent to benefits@nilebyte.example or raised through People Operations. Questions about medical claims should be directed to the insurer's 24-hour hotline printed on the medical card.
